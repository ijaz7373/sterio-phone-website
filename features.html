Home / Features

Everything a handset needs, nothing it doesn’t

A focused set of features for taking calls from your SIM on a phone that doesn’t have one.

Calling

Make and take calls from the iPhone

Native incoming calls

The ring and the full-screen call UI come from iOS itself — not a custom imitation.

Dial pad

A familiar on-screen keypad with a call button and backspace.

Contacts

Search your iPhone contacts and call them in one tap. Names are matched using the last 10 digits, so formats don’t trip it up.

Recents

Incoming, outgoing and missed calls with time and duration. Swipe to delete an entry, or clear everything.

Favorites

Pin the people you call most for one-tap dialing.

Mute and speaker

The in-call screen has mute and speaker toggles, and mute also works from the iOS call UI.

Connection

Pair once, reconnect automatically

QR or code pairing

Scan the QR code on the Android app, or type its short pairing code. Only paired iPhones can connect.

Automatic discovery

The iPhone can find the Android phone on the network by itself, so there’s nothing to type.

Self-healing link

If the connection drops, the iPhone reconnects by itself.

Wi-Fi or hotspot

Works on a shared Wi-Fi network or on the Android phone’s own hotspot — handy when you’re away from home.

Link health

The connection state is visible in the app, and you can share diagnostics from Settings if something misbehaves.

Light on your network

A call uses very little bandwidth, so it works fine on a phone hotspot.

Android host

A quiet app on the phone with the SIM

One-button setup

A guided screen walks through permissions, Shizuku and starting the host. A single Start button turns everything on.

Status tiles

Battery, charging, signal strength, SIM state and network type are sent to the iPhone every 15 seconds.

Mic auto-mute

While a call is bridged, the Android microphone is muted so the room around that phone stays private.

Stays running

Shows a small notification while it’s running so Android keeps it going. The battery-saver exemption is one tap away.

QR pairing screen

Shows a QR code and the short pairing code right in the app.

Compatibility test

One tap checks whether your phone is ready and shows what to fix.

Safety

Guard rails are part of the product

Emergency numbers refused

112, 15, 16, 1122, 911, 999 and other emergency numbers are blocked by the host. Emergency calls must never depend on a relay.

Never recorded

There is no recording feature. Audio is relayed and discarded.

Consent screens

Both apps explain what they access and ask before using the microphone, contacts or camera.
