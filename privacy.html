Privacy policy

How Sterio Phone and this website handle your information.

Last updated 25 November 2026

On this page

  2. What we do not do
  3. This website
  4. Your choices

Sterio Phone lets you use your own iPhone as a remote handset for your own Android phone’s SIM. It has two apps: Sterio Phone (Android host) and Sterio Phone (iPhone).

What the apps do with your data

DataWhat happensWhere it goes
Call audioRelayed live between your two phones so you can talk. Never recorded or stored.Only over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot, directly between the two phones you paired.
Phone numbersShown on the iPhone; the Android host reads the number to forward it.Only between your two phones.
Call historySaved on the iPhone only (number, time, duration, direction) so Recents works. You can delete entries or clear it at any time.Never leaves the iPhone.
Contacts (iPhone)Read on the iPhone to show names and let you choose someone to call.Never uploaded or shared.
Microphone (iPhone)Used only while a call is active, to send your voice into the call.Only to your paired Android phone.
Pairing code & phone addressSaved on your devices so they can reconnect.Stay on your devices.

What we do not do

  • We run no servers for these apps and collect no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no accounts.
  • We do not sell, share or upload your data to anyone.
  • We do not record calls.

This website

This website is static. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images, and does not collect personal data. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

Your choices

  • Stop the Android host or disconnect the iPhone at any time.
  • Delete call history in the iPhone app (Recents → Clear). Uninstalling an app deletes its data.
  • Revoke Contacts, Microphone, Camera or Local Network access in iPhone Settings → Sterio Phone.

Security

The link between your phones stays on your own local network, and a pairing code prevents other devices from connecting. Use a hotspot or Wi-Fi network you trust. See Privacy & security.

Calls and the law

Relaying your own calls to your own device is the apps’ only purpose. Laws about intercepting or recording calls differ by country. Do not use the apps to record or listen to calls of other people without their consent.
