Your calls stay between your phones

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Here’s what that means in practice.

Never recorded

Sterio Phone has no recording feature. Voice is relayed live and discarded. It is not written to storage on either phone.

No servers

We run no relay, cloud or account system. Audio travels directly between the two phones on your own network.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs that phone home. This website sets no cookies either.

Security today

What protects the link

  • Pairing code. Only an iPhone with your code can connect.
  • Your network only. Calls stay on your own Wi-Fi or hotspot — not the public internet.
  • Emergency numbers refused. The host will not dial them, so a relay can never stand between you and help.

About Shizuku: Sterio Phone needs Shizuku, a free open-source helper app, to work with calls. You approve it yourself during setup, and you can remove its access at any time inside Shizuku.

Responsible use

Use it on your own calls

Sterio Phone exists to relay your own calls to your own device. Laws about intercepting or recording calls differ by country, and some regulators restrict SIM-routing and gateway equipment. Do not use Sterio Phone to listen to or record other people’s calls without their consent, and check the rules where you live before relying on it.
