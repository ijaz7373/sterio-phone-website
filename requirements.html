What you’ll need

Sterio Phone works with specific hardware and software. Please read the compatibility notes before you start.

Android phone (with the SIM)

  • Android 12 or newer
  • Developer options and Wireless debugging enabled
  • Shizuku installed and started (how-to guide)
  • A working SIM with calling enabled
  • Connected to Wi-Fi during setup (Wireless debugging needs it); afterwards Wi-Fi or its own hotspot

iPhone (no SIM needed)

  • iOS 17 or newer
  • Microphone, Local Network, Contacts and Camera permissions (Camera only for scanning the QR)
  • Same Wi-Fi as the Android phone, or joined to its hotspot
  • An Apple ID for sideloading during early access

Verified on: a Pixel 9 Pro XL running Android 17. Using a different Android phone? Tell us the model when you request access and we’ll let you know what to expect.

Permissions

What the Android app asks for — and why

PermissionWhy Sterio Phone needs it
Phone stateTo know when a call is ringing, dialing or active.
Call logTo show you the caller’s number.
Answer phone callsSo you can accept a call from the iPhone.
Make phone callsSo the iPhone can start an outgoing call.
NotificationsTo show that Sterio Phone is running.
Ignore battery optimisationsSo Android doesn’t pause the app while the screen is off.
Network & Wi-Fi stateTo connect your two phones on your local network.
