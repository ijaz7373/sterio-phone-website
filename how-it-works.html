Follow a call from the tower to your pocket

Two small apps and your own network. Here’s exactly what happens when someone calls you — and when you call out.

Walkthrough

Two directions, one relay

  1. Someone calls your number

    The call arrives on the Android phone over the normal mobile network, exactly as it always has.

  2. The host app notices and tells your iPhone

    The Sterio Phone app on the Android phone notices the ringing call and tells your iPhone who’s calling.

  3. Your iPhone rings like a phone

    Your iPhone shows the standard iPhone incoming-call screen, with the caller’s name from your contacts.

  4. You tap Accept

    The iPhone tells the Android phone to answer. Voice starts flowing in both directions over your local network, and the Android microphone is muted so the room around that phone isn’t heard.

  5. You hang up from either side

    Ending the call on the iPhone ends it on the Android phone, and the call lands in your Recents with its duration.

  1. Pick a number on the iPhone

    Use the keypad, a contact, a favorite or a recent call.

  2. The iPhone asks the Android phone to dial

    The request goes over the local link. The Android phone places the call with its SIM, using your carrier plan.

  3. The bridge starts while it rings

    You hear the ringing as the call connects.

  4. Talk, then end it

    Either phone can hang up. Emergency numbers are refused by design and must be dialed from a phone with its own SIM.
