Reporting a problem

Help us fix it faster

The more we know, the quicker we can reproduce the issue. Please include:

  • Android model and Android version
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Wi-Fi or hotspot
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • Diagnostics: iPhone app → Settings → Share diagnostics

Quick checks

  • Restarted your Android phone recently? Start Shizuku again, then tap Start host.
  • iPhone app stopped opening after about a week? Ask us for a fresh early-access build.
  • No connection? Confirm both phones are on the same Wi-Fi or the iPhone is on the Android hotspot, and Local Network access is allowed.

Please don’t send pairing codes or Apple ID details by email.
