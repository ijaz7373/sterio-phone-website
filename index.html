The idea

Two phones. One number. No juggling.

Some of us carry a SIM phone and a daily-driver iPhone. Sterio Phone lets the iPhone behave like the handset for the SIM — so you stop carrying, charging and checking two devices.

Rings where you are

An incoming call shows up on the iPhone with the genuine iOS call screen — the caller’s name from your contacts and big Accept and Decline buttons.

Talk both ways

Answer and the iPhone becomes the handset. Your voice goes to the caller; theirs comes to you. The Android phone can stay in a bag or on a charger.

Call out, too

Dial from the keypad, your contacts, favorites or recents on the iPhone. The call leaves from the Android phone using your SIM and your plan.

How a call travels

A short hop over your own network

The carrier call lands on the Android phone like normal. A small host app relays it to your iPhone across Wi-Fi or the Android phone’s hotspot. Nothing is routed through the internet.

What’s inside

Built like a real phone app

Not a web page in a wrapper. The iPhone app uses Apple’s own call screen so calls feel native — and the Android app handles the SIM side.

Native iPhone calling

Calls use the iPhone’s own call screen, so they look and feel like any other call.

Pair in seconds

Scan a QR code or type a short code. The iPhone can also find the Android phone on your network automatically.

Hotspot-friendly

Put the iPhone on the Android phone’s hotspot and take your SIM line anywhere with a signal — no home Wi-Fi required.

History, contacts, favorites

Recents with call duration, names pulled from your iPhone contacts, and favorites you can pin.

Android status at a glance

See the SIM phone’s battery, signal, SIM and network on the iPhone before you leave it somewhere.

Emergency numbers blocked

The host refuses to dial emergency numbers on purpose. Use a phone with its own SIM for emergencies.

Private by design

Your calls never touch our servers — we don’t have any.

Audio flows directly between your two phones on your local network. We run no relay, collect no analytics and ask for no account. Call history lives on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Quick answers

Before you ask

What do I need to use it?

An Android phone with the SIM (Android 12 or newer) and an iPhone running iOS 17 or newer. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi network, or the iPhone joins the Android phone’s hotspot. See requirements for the details and the current compatibility list.

How do I get it?

Download the Android host straight from the early access page, and request the iPhone app by email — we’ll send it with setup help.

Do you record my calls?

No. Audio is streamed live between your phones and is never recorded or stored, and there are no servers in the middle.

How much does it cost?

There’s no pricing yet — early access is free while we test. We’ll announce any pricing well before it applies.
