Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, including the uncomfortable ones.

Getting started

What is Sterio Phone?

A pair of apps that let a SIM-less iPhone act as a remote handset for an Android phone that holds the SIM. Calls ring on the iPhone, you answer, decline and dial from it, and voice travels between the phones over your local network.

What do I need?

An Android phone (Android 12+) with a SIM and an iPhone (iOS 17+), plus a shared Wi-Fi network or the Android phone’s hotspot. See requirements.

Do I need any extra hardware?

No. Only the two phones. There is no box, dongle or Bluetooth bridge.

How much does it cost?

Early access is free while we test. There’s no pricing yet and no paywall. Any future pricing will be announced well in advance.

Calls & audio

Does it work away from home?

Yes, if the iPhone joins the Android phone’s hotspot — then the two phones are on the same network wherever you are.

Can I make emergency calls through it?

No — on purpose. The Android host refuses to dial emergency numbers. Use a phone with its own SIM for emergencies.

Privacy & safety

Do you record my calls?

No. There is no recording feature. Voice is relayed live and never stored.

Do calls go through your servers?

No. We don’t run any. Audio goes straight from phone to phone on your local network.

Is this legal where I live?

Relaying your own calls to your own device is the app’s only purpose, but telecom rules differ by country, and some regulators restrict SIM-routing equipment. Check local rules and get advice before relying on it. This isn’t legal advice.

Android & distribution

Why isn’t the Android app on Google Play?

The Android app relies on a setup step (Shizuku) that Google Play doesn’t distribute, so it’s a direct download from us, with setup help.

What is Shizuku and why is it needed?

Shizuku is a free, open-source helper app that Sterio Phone needs on the Android phone. You install it once, approve Sterio Phone inside it, and can remove access any time. Step-by-step guide.

Why do I need Wi-Fi during setup?

Android only lets you switch on Wireless debugging while the phone is connected to Wi-Fi, and Shizuku needs it to start. So for setup, connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi network, temporarily. See the steps.

Do I need to do anything after my Android phone restarts?

Open Shizuku and start it again, then tap Start host in Sterio Phone. Details.

Which Android phones work?

Sterio Phone is verified on a Pixel 9 Pro XL running Android 17. Tell us your model when you request access and we’ll let you know what to expect.

iPhone app

Why does the iPhone app ask for these permissions?

Microphone to send your voice, Local Network to reach the Android phone, Contacts to show names (optional), and Camera only to scan the pairing QR.

What is the relationship to other apps?

Sterio Phone is an independent project. It’s not affiliated with Apple, Google or any other app or carrier.
