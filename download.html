Try Sterio Phone early

Sterio Phone isn’t on the App Store or Google Play yet. Early access is by request, so we can help you get set up on supported phones.

Request access

Get the Android app now, request the iPhone app

Download the Android app below. For the iPhone app, email us your iPhone model and we’ll send the current build and setup help. The model details help us make sure it works on your phones.

Request the iPhone app

Prefer to write it yourself? Include: Android model and version, iPhone model and iOS version, your country and carrier, and whether you’ll use Wi-Fi or a hotspot.

What you’ll receive

  • The Android host app (APK), straight from this site
  • The iPhone app, installed with your Apple ID during early access
  • A short setup walkthrough and a way to send us diagnostics
  • Updates as we keep improving Sterio Phone

What it costs

Early access is free while we test. There’s no paywall. We’ll announce any future pricing well in advance.
